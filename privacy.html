Privacy Policy

How the Bitloop Lab website handles your information.

Effective date: October 12, 2026

Using one of our Android apps? Each Bitloop Lab app has its own privacy policy that explains exactly what that app collects. See Our apps below. This page covers this website only.

1. Who we are

Bitloop Lab ("we", "us", "our") designs and builds Android apps and websites. This policy explains what information is collected when you visit this website and how it is used.

2. Information we collect

We keep data collection on this website to a minimum. The website has no user accounts, does not use advertising, and does not use analytics or tracking cookies.

  • Messages you send us. The contact form does not send data to our servers. When you press "Send message", it opens your own email app with your name, email address, chosen service, budget and message filled in. We only receive this information if you choose to send that email.
  • Theme preference. If you switch between light and dark mode, that choice is saved in your browser's local storage on your device. It is never sent to us, and you can clear it at any time through your browser settings.
  • Technical data handled by service providers. Like any website, your browser shares basic technical information (such as your IP address and browser type) with the services that deliver the site. See section 4.

3. How we use your information

We use the information in emails you send us only to reply to your enquiry, prepare quotes, and deliver any project you ask us to work on. We do not sell, rent or trade your personal information, and we do not use it for advertising.

4. Third-party services

This website relies on the following services, which may process technical data under their own privacy policies:

  • GitHub Pages hosts this website and may log visitors' IP addresses for security and operational purposes. GitHub Privacy Statement
  • Google Fonts supplies the fonts used on this site, so your browser requests them from Google's servers. Google Privacy Policy
  • Your email provider handles any email you send us through the contact form.

5. Our apps

Our Android apps are separate products with their own privacy policies, which are also linked from each app's Google Play listing. Some of our apps show ads through Google AdMob, which may collect device identifiers and usage data to serve and measure ads. Each app's policy explains this in detail.

    The app-ads.txt file on this website is a public, industry-standard file that tells advertisers which ad networks are authorised to sell ads in our apps. It contains no personal data.

    6. Data retention

    We keep emails from you for as long as needed to respond to you and manage any project we work on together, and to meet any legal or accounting obligations. You can ask us to delete them at any time.

    7. Your rights

    Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct or delete the personal information we hold about you, or to object to how we use it. To make a request, email us at contact@bitlooplab.com. We will respond within a reasonable time.

    8. Children's privacy

    This website is intended for businesses and adults looking for app and web development services. It is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

    9. Security

    This website is served over HTTPS. Because the site does not store any personal data on a server, the main information we hold is email correspondence, which we keep in secure, access-controlled email accounts.

    10. Changes to this policy

    We may update this policy from time to time. When we do, we will change the effective date at the top of this page.

    11. Contact us

    If you have any questions about this privacy policy, email us at contact@bitlooplab.com or use our contact page.
