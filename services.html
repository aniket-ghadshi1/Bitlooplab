FAQ

Good questions.

01How long does it take to build an app?

A simple app usually takes 3–6 weeks. Larger apps with logins, payments or admin panels take longer. We'll give you a clear timeline after our first call.

02How much does an app or website cost?

Every project is different. Share your idea on the contact page and we'll send a free, no-obligation estimate.

03Will you publish the app on Google Play for me?

Yes. We prepare the store listing, screenshots and privacy policy, and handle the full publishing process.

04Can you add ads to my app?

Yes. We integrate Google AdMob and set up the app-ads.txt file on your website so your ad inventory is verified.

05Do I own the source code?

Absolutely. Once the project is complete, you get the full source code and access to all accounts.
